Wholesale and Trade

For the trade

We supply kitchens and counters that are judged on the ingredient. Allocation is planned around your menu and the Italian season rather than a standing price list, and you will deal with the person who bought the thing.

Who We Serve

Three kinds of partner

Restaurants and kitchens

Ristoranti

Standing weekly allocation against your menu, with seasonal items flagged before they land so you can write them in. Whole formats, cut to your spec.

Specialty retail

Gastronomie

Counter and shelf programmes built around a short, defensible list, with the provenance notes your staff need to sell it confidently.

Hotels and private clubs

Alberghi e Circoli

Volume with consistency, across banqueting and à la carte, plus one-off allocation for tastings, festivals and member dinners.

How It Works

Four steps, no catalogue

Step One

Consultation

We talk through what you cook, the volumes you move, your delivery windows and what is currently disappointing you. Usually an hour, and usually with something open on the table.

Step Two

Allocation

We propose a short list against your menu, with the producer and season named for each item and an honest note on which will be intermittent. You taste before anything is committed.

Step Three

Logistics

Cold chain, formats, cut specification, pallet or case, and a delivery rhythm that matches your prep days. Documentation for every consignment, including denomination certificates where they apply.

Step Four

The Standing Order

Your allocation runs on a rhythm, and we come to you when the calendar changes — new oil, truffle opening, a cheese lot worth moving on — rather than waiting to be asked.

Capabilities

What a trade account includes

Everything below comes with the account. None of it is a tier, and none of it costs extra.

  • Named producer, zone and season for every line you buy
  • Denomination certificates on file and available on request
  • Temperature-controlled handling from producer to your door
  • Whole wheels and whole legs, cut and portioned to your specification
  • Advance notice on seasonal openings and closings
  • Staff tastings and provenance notes for front-of-house teams
  • One point of contact, who has met the producers
